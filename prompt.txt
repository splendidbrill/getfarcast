Restructure channel selection into three tiers: weekly content, content ideas, and launch posts


The three tiers

Tier 1 — Weekly Content Engine (full copy-paste posts, generated every week) Channels: Twitter/X, Reddit, LinkedIn Output: Complete platform-native posts, ready to copy and publish Cadence: Generated fresh every week as part of the content engine cycle

Tier 2 — Content Ideas (video concepts and hook scripts, not full posts) Channels: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok Output: Video title + 3-sentence concept + recommended hook + content format suggestion Rationale: We cannot fully automate video content. But the creative direction — what to make, what hook to use — is 60% of the work. This is honest and still genuinely valuable. Cadence: Generated with the playbook, not refreshed weekly (evergreen concepts)

Tier 3 — Launch Posts (one-time posts per platform, separate flow) Channels: Product Hunt, Hacker News (Show HN), Indie Hackers, Betalist, Dev.to, AppSumo, Lobsters/Tildes, GitHub (for open source products) Output: One carefully crafted post per platform, written in that platform's exact native language using the channel knowledge base Cadence: One-time, triggered separately from the content engine via a "Launch your product" CTA in the dashboard
