# GetFarcast Expert Knowledge Base — v1.0
# Research-backed, updated for 2025 LinkedIn algorithm

## Platform Overview
LinkedIn has 1B+ members but only ~1% create content consistently. The feed is chronological + algorithmic. Founders have a massive distribution advantage over companies because personal profiles get 5-10x more organic reach than company pages for the same content.

## Algorithm Mechanics (2025)
- **The "Golden 60 Minutes" rule:** LinkedIn measures engagement in the first hour after posting. High early engagement (likes, comments, shares) gets your post pushed to 2nd and 3rd degree connections. Low early engagement kills it.
- **Comments > Reactions.** A comment is worth 4x a "like" in the algorithm. Your goal is always to generate comments, not just likes.
- **Dwell time matters.** LinkedIn tracks how long people spend reading your post. Longer posts (500-1200 words) that people actually read beat short thin posts.
- **Document/PDF format gets 2-3x reach** of regular text posts. Use it for frameworks, step-by-step guides, case studies.
- **External links kill reach.** NEVER put a URL in a LinkedIn post body. Always put links in the first comment. Always.
- **Best posting times:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8am–10am and 12pm–1pm in your audience's timezone.
- **Post frequency:** 3-5 times per week maximum. Less is more. Quality > quantity.

## Content Formats That Work

### Format 1: The Founder Story Arc (Highest Reach)
**Structure:**
- Line 1 (Hook): A specific, counterintuitive statement or a provocative number
- Lines 2-5: Context — what happened, what you thought would happen vs what did
- Lines 6-10: The insight or lesson
- Last line: A question that invites engagement
**Example Hook:** "We almost killed our startup by being too good at our product."
**Why it works:** Stories stop the scroll. Questions generate comments. Comments train the algorithm.

### Format 2: The Contrarian Take
**Structure:**
- "Unpopular opinion: [something everyone does] is actually wrong."
- Back it up with specific data or real experience
- End with: "Agree or disagree?"
**Why it works:** Disagreement = more comments = more reach. LinkedIn loves controversy (within professional limits).

### Format 3: The "Lessons Learned" List
**Structure:**
- "X things I learned [doing thing] in [timeframe]:"
- Each point is a genuine, specific insight (not platitudes)
- Last point is the most vulnerable or counterintuitive
**Why it works:** Lists are scannable. Founders love validation that others made the same mistakes.

### Format 4: The Document Post (Carousel)
Create a PDF with 5-10 slides. Each slide = 1 insight with a visual.
Slide 1 = Hook (same rules as above)
Slide last = CTA (follow for more, link in comments)
**Why it works:** Gets 2-3x organic reach vs text posts. People share documents more.

### Format 5: The Personal Milestone Post
Share a real metric: first $1K MRR, first 100 users, first enterprise customer.
Be specific. Tell the emotional story around it.
**Why it works:** LinkedIn celebrates professional wins. These posts get shared and commented on widely.

## Connection Strategy
- **Your network IS your distribution.** Connect aggressively with your ICP.
- Send connection requests with a personalized note. Reference something specific from their profile.
- Do NOT pitch in the first message. Ever.
- After 3-5 days of connecting, engage with their content first.
- DM with value before you DM with ask.
- Target: 50 new ICP connections per week for the first 3 months.

## Hashtag Strategy (2025)
- Use 3-5 hashtags maximum. More than 5 hurts reach.
- Mix: 1 broad (#startup), 1 mid-tier (#SaaSgrowth), 1 niche (#bootstrapped)
- Never use generic spam hashtags: #love, #success, #motivation
- Put hashtags at the END of the post, not inline

## What Kills LinkedIn Reach
- External links in the post body (always comment)
- Stock photos (low authenticity signal)
- Company page posts without personal amplification
- Generic advice that sounds like a LinkedIn coach wrote it
- Posting and disappearing — you must respond to every comment in the first 30 minutes

## Anti-Slop Content Rules for LinkedIn
- Open with a hook that makes someone stop scrolling. Test: "Would I stop scrolling for this?"
- Use short paragraphs — 1-3 sentences max per paragraph. White space is critical.
- Never use: "I'm thrilled to announce," "delighted to share," "proud to say," "passionate about"
- DO use: Specific numbers, real quotes, vulnerability about failure, honest opinions
- Format numbers with commas and units: "3,400 users" not "thousands of users"
