# Hacker News Distribution Playbook
# GetFarcast Expert Knowledge Base — v1.0

## Platform Overview
Hacker News is run by Y Combinator. It has ~5-10M monthly readers, almost entirely technical founders, engineers, VCs, and product people. It's the highest-quality technical audience on the internet. A successful "Show HN" launch can drive 500-5000 visitors in a single day.

## Understanding the HN Culture
- HN users are allergic to marketing language. They can smell a pitch from a mile away.
- They respect technical depth, honest vulnerability, and builders who've thought hard about a problem.
- They will brutally critique your product in the comments — and that is VALUABLE feedback, not attacks.
- The community values substance over polish. A raw demo beats a slick landing page.

## Algorithm Mechanics
- **Upvotes in the first hour determine front page placement.**
- Comments with good engagement help too, but the comment/upvote quality matters.
- **Time decay is aggressive.** A post from 5 hours ago is essentially invisible.
- **Flagging system:** If your post gets flagged as spam/self-promo by enough users, it's buried. Never sound like marketing.
- **Best time to post:** Thursday 9am-11am EST. This is peak HN traffic.
- Second best: Tuesday or Wednesday, same time window.

## Show HN Format (The Launch Post)
This is the standard format for launching a product on HN.

**Title format:** "Show HN: [Product Name] – [What it does in plain English]"
Examples of good titles:
- "Show HN: I built a tool that turns your Reddit comments into warm leads"
- "Show HN: An open-source alternative to HubSpot for indie hackers"
- "Show HN: I automated the process of finding podcast guests for my show"

**Bad title examples:**
- "Show HN: GetFarcast – Your AI-Powered Growth Engine" (too marketing-speak)
- "Show HN: Check out my new SaaS tool" (too vague)

**The First Comment (Critical)**
The first comment on a Show HN appears at the TOP for every visitor. This is your chance to tell the full story.
Include:
1. Why you built this (the personal pain point)
2. What it does (technical, specific — not marketing speak)
3. What's NOT working yet (shows honesty)
4. What you specifically want feedback on
Keep it under 400 words. Make it human.

## Ask HN Format (Pre-Launch Research)
"Ask HN: How do you currently [solve the problem your product solves]?"
This is underused by founders. Do this BEFORE building. You get:
- Real data on how people currently solve the problem
- Organic interest from people who have this pain
- A community you can go back to when you launch

## What Gets You Flagged/Buried
- Any marketing language (even subtle)
- Posting from a brand new HN account (create an account months before you need it)
- Asking for upvotes anywhere
- Not engaging with comments (if you launch and don't reply, HN notices)
- Duplicate posts or cross-posts from other platforms

## Comment Engagement Strategy
When your Show HN is live:
- Be online and responding within minutes for the first 2-3 hours
- Thank people for criticism. "That's a fair critique, here's how we're thinking about it..."
- If someone makes a good point, say so explicitly. HN respects intellectual honesty.
- Never be defensive about your product

## CAC Estimate
- Organic Show HN: $0 cash, 2-4 hours of preparation
- Time-to-traffic: Same day as post (if it hits front page)
- Conversion from HN traffic: 1-5% (very high intent)
- Time-to-paying-user: Within 48-72 hours of a good Show HN
- Realistic outcome: 100-2000 visitors, 5-50 signups from a good launch
