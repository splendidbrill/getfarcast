# YouTube Distribution Playbook
# GetFarcast Expert Knowledge Base — v1.0

## Platform Overview
YouTube has 2.5B+ monthly active users and is the #2 search engine in the world. For founders, it's often overlooked but is one of the highest-ROI channels long-term because YouTube content has a "long tail" — a video can generate leads for years after it's published.

## Algorithm Mechanics (2025)
- **Click-through rate (CTR) on thumbnail + title determines initial push.** A good CTR is 4-10%. Below 2% = YouTube stops showing it.
- **Watch time and audience retention are equally critical.** Average retention below 30% signals a poor video. Above 50% = YouTube amplifies it.
- **YouTube Shorts get distributed aggressively to non-subscribers.** Best format for rapid channel growth in 2025.
- **SEO matters.** YouTube is a search engine. Titles, descriptions, and tags affect discoverability for months/years.
- **Upload frequency affects channel growth velocity.** 1 video/week minimum for growth phase. 3-4/week if including Shorts.

## Content Formats That Work

### Format 1: "How I Built [X]" / Founder Documentary
The most powerful format for founders. Show your full journey: the problem, the build, the failures, the wins.
Structure:
- Minute 0-1: Hook (show the end result first — "I went from $0 to $5K MRR in 8 weeks building this")
- Minute 1-5: The problem/backstory
- Minute 5-15: The build process (screen recordings, talking head)
- Minute 15-20: What worked, what didn't
- End: CTA (try the product, subscribe)
Why it works: Founders have earned-media stories. This format requires no budget, just a camera and honesty.

### Format 2: The Tutorial / "How to Do X"
Find the exact questions your ICP is searching for. Make videos that answer them concretely.
Example: If your product helps with cold email, make "How to write cold emails that get replies" — not "Introducing [Product Name]."
The product is the TOOL you use to solve the problem in the tutorial, not the subject.
Why it works: Search discovery means people are actively looking for this. High intent.

### Format 3: YouTube Shorts (15-60 seconds)
Use for: Quick tips, hot takes, behind-the-scenes glimpses, repurposed TikTok content.
Hook must land in first 0-3 seconds.
If a Short performs well (>100K views), it drives people to your long-form content.

### Thumbnail Formula
- Use your face if possible (faces with strong expressions get 30-50% higher CTR)
- Bold text overlay: 3-5 words maximum
- High contrast colors — no pastels
- Create thumbnail FIRST before writing the video title. Let the thumbnail drive the hook.

### Title Formula
- Include the primary search keyword early
- Create curiosity or promise a specific outcome
- Use numbers: "5 things," "in 30 days," "$0 to $10K"
- A/B test titles: YouTube Studio allows you to test multiple titles

## What Kills YouTube Growth
- Talking for the first 3 minutes before getting to the point (retention collapses)
- Poor audio quality (viewers tolerate bad video, not bad audio — get a $50 mic)
- No call to action or weak CTAs
- Uploading once and disappearing
- Making videos about your product instead of about your customer's problems
- Ignoring YouTube Analytics (it tells you exactly where people drop off)

## Finding Your First 100 Subscribers
- Share every video on Twitter, LinkedIn, and Reddit (relevant subreddits)
- Reply to comments immediately (the first 24 hours matter)
- Collab with another small channel in your niche
- Pin a comment with a CTA or additional resource

## Anti-Slop Content Rules for YouTube
- Hook = the most interesting/useful/surprising thing in your video. Show it IN THE FIRST 30 SECONDS.
- Do not use an intro sequence for more than 5 seconds. Nobody watches intros.
- Cut dead air ruthlessly in editing. Jump cuts are fine.
- Add captions/subtitles. 80% of YouTube videos are watched in non-silent environments.
- Never say "smash that like button" unironically — it signals low-quality content.

## CAC Estimate
- Organic tutorial content: $0 cash, 4-8 hours per video
- Time-to-traffic from a good informational video: Days to weeks (SEO builds over time)
- Time-to-first-paying-user from YouTube: 4-12 weeks of consistent uploads
- Long-tail value: A great video can generate leads for 12-36 months after publishing
- YouTube Ads: $0.05-0.30 per view; $20-100 CPL for tutorials with clear CTA
