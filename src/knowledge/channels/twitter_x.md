# X (Twitter) Distribution Playbook
# GetFarcast Expert Knowledge Base — v1.0

## Platform Overview
X (Twitter) has ~250M daily active users but organic reach is increasingly pay-to-play. However, for technical founders and startup products, X remains one of the best platforms for building a personal brand and finding early adopters through the "build in public" movement.

## Algorithm Mechanics (2025)
- **X Premium (Blue checkmark) accounts get 2-4x more reach.** Worth it if Twitter is your primary channel.
- **Engagement in the first 30 minutes is critical.** Early likes/replies/retweets signal quality to the algorithm.
- **Reply engagement is amplified.** When you reply to tweets from large accounts in your niche, your reply gets seen by their entire audience.
- **Threads get more reach than single tweets** when engagement is high. They also get bookmarked (bookmarks are a strong signal).
- **Best posting times:** Monday–Friday, 8am–10am EST and 5pm–7pm EST.
- **Avoid posting on weekends** unless your audience is consumer-focused.

## Content Formats That Work

### Format 1: Build in Public Updates
Daily or weekly real updates about what you're building, shipping, learning.
"Week 3 of building [product]:
→ Shipped: [X]
→ Learned: [Y]
→ Struggling with: [Z]
MRR: $0 → $200"
Why it works: People root for you. They feel invested in your journey. They share your wins.

### Format 2: The Insight Thread
- Tweet 1: Bold claim or counterintuitive insight (the hook)
- Tweets 2-8: Expand each point with specific evidence, examples, or data
- Tweet 9: Summary + CTA
Why it works: Threads get bookmarked. Bookmarks train the algorithm to show your content to more people.

### Format 3: The Hot Take
Single tweet with a strong, specific opinion about something in your industry.
"Most SaaS founders are building the wrong thing. Here's how to tell if you are."
Why it works: Replies (including disagreement) = engagement = reach. Controversial but thoughtful opinions get shared.

### Format 4: The Reply Strategy
Go to tweets from large accounts in your space (10K-500K followers). Write the most insightful, specific reply you can. Don't be sycophantic — add genuine value or a counterpoint.
Why it works: You show up in front of their entire audience for $0.

## What Kills X Reach
- Tweeting links in every post (X suppresses external links heavily)
- Mass following / mass DM (gets you shadowbanned)
- Posting without engaging with others (it's a conversation, not a broadcast)
- Generic motivational content "hustle harder" type tweets
- Inconsistency (tweet 20 times then disappear for a week)

## Build In Public Playbook
This is the most effective founder-led strategy on X:
1. Tweet BEFORE your product is ready
2. Share your thought process, your doubts, your iterations
3. Ask your audience for input on decisions ("Should we charge $19 or $49?")
4. Share failures transparently ("We thought X would work. It didn't. Here's why.")
5. Celebrate small wins publicly ("First paying customer who isn't a friend!")
People who follow your journey become your most loyal customers and loudest promoters.

## Anti-Slop Content Rules for X
- First tweet in a thread must be STRONG. If it doesn't stop the scroll, nobody reads the thread.
- Use line breaks between ideas. Dense text on X is ignored.
- Numbers grab attention: "7 things," "I spent $0 on this," "$50K in 6 months"
- Never start with "I" as the first word (tested to underperform vs other openers)
- Never use: "Excited to announce," "thrilled to share," "game-changer"
- Do not end every tweet with "RT if you agree" — it looks desperate

## CAC Estimate
- Organic content (threads, build-in-public): $0 cash, 1 hour/day
- Reply strategy: $0 cash, 30 min/day
- Time-to-first-follower-from-content: Immediate if thread goes viral
- Time-to-first-paying-customer from X: 4-8 weeks of consistent building
- X Ads: $5-20 CPL for B2C; $30-80 for B2B SaaS
