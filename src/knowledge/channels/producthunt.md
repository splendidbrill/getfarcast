# Product Hunt Distribution Playbook
# GetFarcast Expert Knowledge Base — v1.0

## Platform Overview
Product Hunt is THE launch platform for tech products. A #1 ranking can drive 5,000-20,000 visitors in a single day. However, the launch windows are short (from 12:01am PST) and competition is fierce. A well-prepared PH launch is a major moment for any SaaS or app.

## Algorithm Mechanics
- Voting happens between 12:01am–11:59pm PST. Zero votes carry over.
- Early upvotes (first 2-4 hours) are weighted more heavily.
- PH's algorithm also detects "suspicious" voting (e.g., all votes from new accounts), which can get posts flagged.
- Featured products are selected by PH editors — having a well-known hunter helps.

## Pre-Launch Preparation (2-4 Weeks Before)

### Build a subscriber list
Product Hunt lets you create an "upcoming" page before you launch. Use it to collect people who want to be notified. This is your launch-day army.
- Share the upcoming page in your newsletter, social media, and relevant communities.
- Target: 200+ subscribers before launch day.

### Choose your Hunter
A "Hunter" is someone who submits your product on PH. A high-follower hunter can put your product in front of their audience immediately.
- Look for hunters with 5K+ followers on Product Hunt.
- Reach out 3-4 weeks in advance via Twitter/LinkedIn DM.
- Do not ask your maker friends who have 20 followers — it wastes a significant advantage.

### Set your launch date carefully
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.
- Avoid: Mondays (competition from weekend buildup), Fridays (lower traffic), holiday weeks.
- Check what other products are launching the same week (PH shows upcoming launches).

## Launch Day Playbook (Hour by Hour)

### 12:01am PST — GO LIVE
- You or your hunter submits at exactly 12:01am PST.
- Immediately post in your Launch Day Swarm (Slack/Discord/WhatsApp group you prepared).

### 6am-10am PST — Push hard
- Email your subscriber list: "We just launched on Product Hunt. If you've found our product valuable, your upvote means the world."
- Post on LinkedIn, Twitter, your communities.
- Ask for HONEST support, never ask friends to "upvote." Ask them to "check it out and upvote if you find it genuinely useful."

### All day — Engage on PH
- Respond to every comment on your PH listing.
- Answer questions thoroughly and quickly.
- Thank support, take criticism graciously.

## The First Comment (Make or Break)
The maker comment is pinned at the top of your PH listing. Write it like a founder, not a copywriter.
Include:
1. The single pain point you built this for
2. How it works in 2-3 sentences
3. What's the pricing (PH users hate discovering a $200/month surprise)
4. Current limitations (honesty builds trust)
5. What feedback you most want today

## What Gets You Ranked Lower
- Fake upvotes from VPNs or brand new PH accounts (gets flagged)
- Launching and then disappearing (no maker engagement)
- A mediocre Product Description (spend 2 hours on the tagline)
- No video demo (products with demo videos rank ~40% higher)
- Submitting on a Saturday or Monday (bad timing)

## Post-Launch Follow-Up
- Write a "How our Product Hunt launch went" blog post or LinkedIn post within 48 hours. These get shared widely.
- Email all commenters 1 week later with an update on what you changed based on their feedback.
- Follow every person who upvoted you on Twitter/LinkedIn.

## CAC Estimate
- A good PH launch (top 5 of the day): $0 cash, 3-4 weeks of preparation
- Realistic upvotes for a prepared launch: 300-800
- Traffic from a top 5 finish: 3,000-15,000 visitors in 24 hours
- Signups: typically 2-5% of traffic for B2C, 1-3% for B2B
- Time-to-paying-user: Same day if freemium; within 1-2 weeks for paid
